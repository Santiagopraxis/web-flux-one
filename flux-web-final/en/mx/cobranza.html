PRODUCT: COLLECTION

Every invoice you send deserves to get paid.
What if it always did?

Connect your invoices with the payment methods enabled for your company. fluX One facilitates sending instructions, queries statuses reported by providers, and reconciles payment information.

What makes collection smart?

Payment Method Integration

Connect the payment methods contracted by your company through a single technology integration.

Real-time Reconciliation

Automatically match payments with invoices to know who paid instantly, without manual intervention or human error.

Predictive Behavior

Analytical models that identify payment patterns to estimate portfolio behavior and prioritize collection actions.

WhatsApp Collection Agent

Your automated agent that sends personalized reminders, answers common invoice questions, and processes payments directly in the chat, friction-free.

PCI-DSS Tokenization

Facilitates subsequent payments using identifiers generated and managed by the contracted tokenization provider within its certified security environment.

Recurring Charges

Sends reminders and facilitates access to the payment flow. Recurring charges are executed according to the amount, frequency, and term authorized by the cardholder or account holder, with cancellation mechanisms. Tokenization does not replace that authorization.
